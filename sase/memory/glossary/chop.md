A job is one short, script-only unit of AXE automation. AXE supplies its context and
applies configured cadence, triggers, guards, timeouts, and deduplication; a structured
result may request follow-up launches, which only the runner performs. Chop remains an
accepted lookup alias for older documentation, commands, and legacy state paths.
